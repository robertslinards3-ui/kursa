## Recurring obligations during execution

- **At least weekly:** contact with the Darba vadītājs.
- **At least monthly:** Darba vadītājs + team update LU IAC on progress.
- **Immediately (within 3 working days of the team contract, sooner in spirit):** tell the
  Darba vadītājs and LU IAC in writing about any delay risk, team-composition change, or
  anything else that could affect delivery.
- **Interim report** (template: nolikums appendix 6 / `6. pielikums`), due **2027-01-14** —
  covers progress vs. plan, training/events attended, networking, team collaboration.
  Followed by an **interim presentation on 2027-01-21**.
- **Final report** (template: appendix 7), due **2027-02-18** — idea-progress vs. plan (as a
  %), prototype description, methods used, future potential, individual self-assessments,
  team collaboration assessment, Darba vadītājs/expert collaboration assessment, overall
  programme assessment, budget-vs-results fit. Scored out of 24 points by committee;
  **≥16 needed to pass**.
- Every deliverable goes to the Darba vadītājs for sign-off **before** it's submitted to
  LU IAC — don't send anything straight to LU without that step.
- **Demo Day:** public presentation of results to the committee — mandatory, also the
  trigger for the prize-fund judging.
- **Before and after the programme:** an individual self-assessment survey via Microsoft
  Forms (innovation/entrepreneurship skills) — deadline announced separately by LU IAC.
- Team changes (someone leaving/joining) → written notice to Darba vadītājs + LU IAC
  immediately; may require a contract amendment.
- If the team starts real economic activity / registers a company during the programme →
  immediately notify Darba vadītājs + LU IAC in writing (triggers a *de minimis* state-aid
  review).

## Monthly stipend mechanics (per team member, optional, €280/mo, max 3 months)

Separate from the project budget above — this is personal income support, applied for
individually, not automatic. **60 stipends total, competitively awarded** — applying doesn't
guarantee getting one.

1. Submit a free-form application to **inovaciju.granti@lu.lv** by **2026-11-13** (name,
   personal ID, bank account (IBAN), declared address, institution, programme track,
   student card number, contact details) — see nolikums §53 for the exact field list.
2. Submit a monthly report by each deadline — **2026-12-27, 2027-01-27, 2027-02-27** —
   covering: name, team name, number and dates of meetings with the Darba vadītājs, at
   least one skill/piece of knowledge gained and how it ties to the team's application,
   planned-vs-actual tasks and results for the period (with evidence), and next period's
   planned tasks.
3. Scored 0–6 by the stipend committee (meeting attendance ≥75%, work matches plan, Darba
   vadītājs's assessment of individual contribution) — **≥5/6 needed** to get paid for that
   month. Missing one month's report doesn't forfeit future months. Stipends only start
   paying from the programme's 2nd month.
4. Paid out ~10 working days after a positive committee decision, to the IBAN given.
